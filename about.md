I am a PhD student in the ERC-Project ASSISTED LAB at the MED-HSG School of Medicine, University of St. Gallen. I am part of the Chair for Medical Humanities under Prof. Dr. Anna Elsner and the DOK-Programme Organisation Studies and Cultural Theory.

I studied Educational Sciences (B.A.) and Sociology (M.A.) at LMU München and spent a semester at Kyushu University in Fukuoka, Japan. From 2019 to 2025 I worked at the Institute of Sociology at LMU München, in the DFG-Project "Vom 'Guten Sterben'" at the Chair for General Theory under Prof. Dr. Armin Nassehi.

My research covers palliative care and dying, the role of organizations and professions in care settings, and the sociology of discourse, including antisemitic narratives in protest discourse. I have also worked on student preferences and perceived fairness in examination formats.
